AVIP GPSCam – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

AVIP GPSCam ("the app") is a free camera app published by AVIP Business Solutions LLP ("AVIP", "we", "us"), AIIMS–Naubatpur New Nahar Road, Patna – 801109, Bihar, India. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us at registration

When you first open the app, it asks you to register once with:

  • your name,
  • your company name,
  • your mobile number, and
  • whether you agree to receive information about our products and services (optional tick box).

Along with these, the app sends your phone model, Android version, the app version and a random installation ID created by the app (not linked to any phone identifier). This information is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a Google Sheet owned by AVIP, using a Google Apps Script web service.

Why: to know who uses the app, to provide support, and – only if you ticked the consent box – to contact you about AVIP's products and services (for example, solar installations). We do not contact you for marketing if you did not give consent.

2. Camera, microphone and location

PermissionWhat it is used for
CameraTaking photos and recording videos.
MicrophoneRecording sound with videos. If you deny it, videos are recorded without sound.
Location (precise and approximate)Stamping your location (map, address, latitude and longitude) on your photos and videos, and adding GPS details to the photo file. Location is used only while the app is open; it is never used in the background.
Motion sensorsShowing the level line and the north arrow / direction the camera faces.

Your photos, videos and your location are not uploaded to AVIP. Photos and videos are saved only on your phone, in the Pictures/GPSMapCamera folder, and are shared only when you choose to share them.

3. Services that receive your location

  • Map image: to draw the small map, the app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap. The tile request reveals the approximate area being shown and your IP address to the OpenStreetMap servers (OpenStreetMap privacy policy).
  • Address: the address is looked up with your phone's built-in Android geocoding service, which on most phones is provided by Google (Google privacy policy).

4. What we do not do

  • No advertisements and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not access photos or files other than those the app itself saved.

5. Sharing

Registration details are stored in AVIP's Google account (Google acts as our service provider) and are used only by AVIP. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep registration details for as long as they are needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete them.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can deny any permission in your phone's settings; the related feature will then not work.
  • You can delete photos and videos at any time from the app's gallery or your phone's gallery.
  • To stop marketing messages, or to have your registration details deleted, email contact@avipsolar.in or call +91 92880 76289 with the mobile number you registered. We will delete your details within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults and businesses. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

Registration details are sent over HTTPS and stored in a private Google Sheet that only AVIP can access.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
